Problem Statement & Technical Innovation
Overcoming Acoustic Chaos and Mesh Scalability Limits

1. The Acoustic Chaos Problem

Traditional meeting tools broadcast flat voice globally. In a 30-person room, multiple conversations cannot coexist; users talk over each other.

ProxiSpeak Solution

Dynamic distance attenuation (Linear, Inverse, Exponential) with a $100\,\text{px}$ earshot boundary and $120\,\text{px}$ smooth silence cutoff.

2. Full Mesh Bandwidth Explosion

Full WebRTC mesh scales at $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$. At 20 users, each laptop must stream 19 audio feeds, crashing CPU and bandwidth.

Connection Capper (N=4)

Limits active peer connections to $N=4$ nearest avatars with $15\,\text{px}$ preemption hysteresis, eliminating connection flapping.

Acoustic Occlusion & Wall Loss

Ray-casting against 2D AABBs: desks attenuate by $-6.0\,\text{dB}$ ($\times 0.501$), walls by $-14.0\,\text{dB}$ ($\times 0.199$). Low-pass cut-off drops from $20\,\text{kHz}$ to $400-900\,\text{Hz}$.

Clickless Parameter Scheduling

Avoids audio pops via linearRampToValueAtTime over a $50\,\text{ms}$ smoothing window, ensuring seamless voice fading during movement.
